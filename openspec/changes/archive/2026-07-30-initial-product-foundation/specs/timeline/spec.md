## Purpose

Provides a public chronological content feed where visitors can discover available posts.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public timeline access

The system SHALL allow anonymous visitors to view public timeline content.

#### Scenario: Anonymous visitor views timeline
- **WHEN** an anonymous visitor accesses the timeline
- **THEN** the system SHALL display public posts chronologically


### Requirement: Timeline ordering

The system SHALL display posts ordered from newest to oldest.

#### Scenario: User views recent posts
- **WHEN** posts are displayed in the timeline
- **THEN** newer posts SHALL appear before older posts
